# E-E-A-T Audit Rubric

Expanded scoring guide for Stage 8 of `SKILL.md`. Score each category
`0`, `1`, or `2`. Maximum total: **20 points**.

## Scoring anchors

| Score | Meaning | Typical evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Missing or poor | No attempt, or actively misleading |
| 1 | Partial | Present but generic, unverified, or inconsistent |
| 2 | Strong and verified | Specific, sourced, and honest about limits |

## Categories

### 1. Search intent

- **2** — Answers the real query in the first screen; matches the
  dominant SERP intent.
- **1** — Relevant but buries the answer or mixes intents.
- **0** — Off-intent or targets a different query.

### 2. Helpfulness

- **2** — Adds original structure, examples, or decision support beyond
  generic summaries.
- **1** — Accurate but interchangeable with existing pages.
- **0** — Filler or restated definitions only.

### 3. Experience

- **2** — Genuine, approved first-hand detail, or the absence is
  explicitly disclosed.
- **1** — Vague gestures toward experience without evidence.
- **0** — Fabricated or implied experience.

### 4. Expertise

- **2** — Real author, accurate credentials, and documented clinician
  review.
- **1** — Author identified but credentials or review unverified.
- **0** — Invented or misassigned expertise.

### 5. Authority

- **2** — Credible, current institutional and professional sources.
- **1** — Mixed-quality sources or reliance on manufacturer material.
- **0** — No credible sourcing or fabricated authority.

### 6. Trust

- **2** — Transparent ownership, dates, risks, limitations, and
  commercial relationships.
- **1** — Some transparency gaps.
- **0** — Hidden risks, fake reviews, or misleading claims.

### 7. Accuracy

- **2** — Every important claim is verifiable and correctly scoped.
- **1** — Minor unsupported or overgeneralized claims.
- **0** — Material inaccuracies.

### 8. On-page SEO

- **2** — Clean metadata, hierarchy, slug, alt text, and internal links.
- **1** — Minor issues that do not block comprehension.
- **0** — Broken hierarchy or metadata.

### 9. Readability

- **2** — Clear, scannable, jargon explained, no filler.
- **1** — Dense or repetitive in places.
- **0** — Hard to follow or heavily padded.

### 10. Conversion

- **2** — Relevant CTA that respects the reader.
- **1** — Weak or slightly pushy CTA.
- **0** — Misleading, pressured, or fear-based CTA.

## Hard stops (override the score)

Any of the following means **do not publish**, regardless of total:

- Fabricated source, credential, testimonial, case study, or experience.
- Unsupported high-impact medical claim.
- Missing required clinician review (`NOT MEDICALLY APPROVED`).
- Privacy or consent violation involving patient material.
- Guarantee, permanence, or "suitable for everyone" claim.
- Structured data that misrepresents visible page content.
